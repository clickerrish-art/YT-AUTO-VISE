SonicVhibez YouTube Automation

SonicVhibez YouTube Automation supports authorized publishing operations for the SonicVhibez YouTube channel, including preparing and scheduling approved videos, uploading content with the channel owner's authorization, and reading channel/video information for operational verification.

The application is intended for SonicVhibez only. It is independent and is not affiliated with Google or YouTube. Content must be authorized and rights-cleared before publication.

Google and YouTube access

When the channel owner grants permission, the application uses YouTube upload access for authorized uploads and read-only access for channel and video checks. Access may be revoked through Google Account security settings.
