California Spiny Lobster: No Big Front Claws?

**Description**

Meet the California spiny lobster (*Panulirus interruptus*), a rocky-reef crustacean with long antennae and no large front pincers. Unlike clawed lobsters, it relies on its spiny body and a powerful tail-flip escape. It shelters in reef crevices by day and forages after dark.
