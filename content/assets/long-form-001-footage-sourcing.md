## Required before any clip is accepted

2. Record the original filename, source URL, contributor, download timestamp, dimensions, duration, frame rate, codecs, and SHA-256.

6. Assign a specific narration beat and source in/out; do not loop footage to fill runtime.
7. Keep the original immutable and record any derived edit mapping.
