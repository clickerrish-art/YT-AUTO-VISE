## Description draft

A river can look empty while a Nile crocodile remains almost completely submerged.

This wildlife documentary explores how the Nile crocodile (*Crocodylus niloticus*) uses its waterline posture, specialised skin sensory organs and ambush behaviour. We look at what research has demonstrated about sensitivity to water-surface waves, how crocodiles respond to more than one sensory signal, and where the evidence stops short of proving a particular wild hunting event.

The goal is to understand the animal, not turn it into a monster story. Crocodiles are powerful wild predators. Never approach a crocodile or enter water known to contain crocodilians. Follow local wildlife authority advice and warning signs.
