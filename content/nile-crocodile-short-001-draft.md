## Narration

A river can look completely still. But beneath the surface, a Nile crocodile may be watching.

Notice how little of its head needs to break the water.

Its eyes, ears and nostrils sit high on its skull, allowing much of its body to remain submerged.

And when it swims, its powerful tail provides most of the forward thrust.

But there is more to this animal than its ambush-hunting reputation.

Young crocodiles feed on much smaller prey than adults, and females can protect their hatchlings.

The Nile crocodile is an extraordinary African predator.

Respect its habitat. Never approach the water's edge carelessly.

Discover the wild with SonicVhibez.
