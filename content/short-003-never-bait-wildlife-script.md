## Narration (approximately 125 words)

A dramatic wildlife shot is never worth putting an animal at risk.

Baiting can change an animal's natural behaviour. It can draw predators toward people, roads, or places where animals may be harmed. And when several animals gather around food, competition can turn dangerous.

The same rule applies behind the camera: don't chase an animal, block its escape route, or get closer just to fill the frame.

Instead, use a longer lens, stay in a vehicle or at a safe legal distance, and let the animal decide what happens next. Record natural sound. Hold the shot. Give the moment room to unfold.

Responsible wildlife filmmaking isn't about controlling nature. It's about being patient enough to witness it.

Protect the animal first. The story comes second.
