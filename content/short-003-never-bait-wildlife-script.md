## Narration (read calmly; final duration must be measured)

A dramatic wildlife shot is never worth putting an animal at risk.

Baiting can change where animals go and how they behave. Follow local wildlife guidance, and never lure animals toward people or roads.

The same rule applies behind the camera: don't chase an animal, block its escape route, or get closer just to fill the frame.

Instead, use a longer lens and keep a safe, lawful distance. Let the animal decide what happens next. Record natural sound. Hold the shot. Give the moment room to unfold.

Responsible wildlife filmmaking isn't about controlling nature. It's about being patient enough to witness it.

Protect the animal first. The story comes second.
