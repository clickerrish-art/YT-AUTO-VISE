## Render gate

1. Generate final narration and captions from the approved script.
2. Select assets against this shotlist and complete the asset register.
3. Verify factual claims and that each visual supports the narration.
4. Record actual asset paths and evidence in the render manifest.
5. Render locally or in a controlled workflow; validate duration, resolution, audio, captions, and black frames.
6. Human-review the complete export and thumbnail.
7. Keep initial YouTube upload private. Public publishing remains disabled until channel identity, rights, metadata, disclosures, and final approval are confirmed.

This file is a planning aid; it does not itself clear footage, create finished video, upload, or publish.
