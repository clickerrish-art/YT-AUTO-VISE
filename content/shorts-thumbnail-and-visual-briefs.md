# SonicVhibez Shorts — Thumbnail and Visual Direction

These are production briefs, not rendered images. Use only original footage, properly licensed stock, or generated assets with commercial-use rights. Do not imply a staged encounter is wild documentary footage.

## Short 001: How to Make Better Wildlife Videos for YouTube

**Thumbnail concept:** A photorealistic African elephant in natural golden-hour light, framed on the right third; softly blurred savanna background; clean documentary color grading; a small camera silhouette in the lower left to signal wildlife filmmaking.

**Thumbnail text:** PLAN THE SHOT

**Composition:** Vertical 9:16 safe framing for Shorts cover crops. Keep the elephant's eye and trunk clear of interface overlays. Use no logos, watermarks, fake danger, or exaggerated expressions.

**Visual sequence (55 seconds):**
- 0–4s: Immediate close wildlife detail; title text appears briefly.
- 4–14s: Wide habitat shot, then a stable medium shot.
- 14–26s: Patient observation from a safe distance; never show baiting or pursuit.
- 26–38s: Natural behavior, framed steadily; brief edit-timeline insert if licensed.
- 38–50s: Readable captions and a music-rights reminder.
- 50–55s: Calm end frame with “Wildlife Creator's Vault — link in description.”

**Alt text:** Elephant in golden-hour savanna beside a wildlife-filming theme.

## Short 002: 3 Steps to Plan a Wildlife Documentary Short

**Thumbnail concept:** A photorealistic leopard resting alertly on a branch in a natural habitat, framed on the upper-right area with strong but realistic contrast and enough negative space for text. Use licensed footage/image only.

**Thumbnail text:** 3 STEPS TO PLAN

**Composition:** Documentary photography, natural anatomy and lighting, no composite predator-prey encounter, no graphic content, no fake urgency. Maintain legibility at small size.

**Visual sequence (55 seconds):**
- 0–4s: Clear animal subject with one-line hook.
- 4–15s: Subject and question; use habitat footage that matches the species.
- 15–27s: Simple visual story arc: hook, behavior, explanation.
- 27–39s: Show footage-selection/editing process; do not splice clips to misrepresent chronology.
- 39–50s: Rights checklist, captions, fact-checking.
- 50–55s: Calm end frame with “Wildlife Creator's Vault — link in description.”

**Alt text:** Leopard in natural habitat beside a concise wildlife-documentary planning theme.

## Release gate

Before export, verify the selected still/footage licence permits commercial use and YouTube use, verify any music licence, review factual claims, and compare captions against final narration. These briefs do not certify asset rights or make either Short publish-ready.
