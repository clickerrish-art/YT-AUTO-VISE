## Qualitative search-intent evidence

- [BC SPCA — Ethical wildlife photography](https://spca.bc.ca/about-us/our-work/wild-animal-programs/ethical-wildlife-photography-how-photographers-can-keep-themselves-and-animals-safe/): distances vary by species; use binoculars or a zoom lens; back away when animals show stress.
- [Mammal Society — Ethical wildlife photography for mammals](https://mammal.org.uk/position-statements/ethicalwildlifephotography): use long lenses and avoid disturbing natural behaviour or habitat.
- [Audubon — Ethical bird photography and videography](https://www.audubon.org/photography/awards/audubons-guide-ethical-bird-photography-and-videography): covers video as well as photography and discourages disturbance, baiting and habitat damage.

The search evidence supports the Short's ethical-filming angle. It does not establish a universal safe distance; the script correctly tells creators to follow species- and location-specific rules.
