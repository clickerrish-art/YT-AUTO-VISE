## Review of the 2025 multisensory study

The script's description is appropriately qualified: it calls the work a controlled study of young Nile crocodiles, describes the relative timing of airborne sound and water-surface waves, and explicitly says it does not prove that every adult crocodile uses the same cue sequence during a wild hunt.

The source reports experiments with 19 naïve juvenile Nile crocodiles tested individually in a controlled basin at Crocoparc in Agadir, Morocco. It reports preferential movement toward the water-wave source under the tested conditions, irrespective of which stimulus arrived first when the delay remained within a few seconds. This is not direct footage or field proof of a wild ambush.
