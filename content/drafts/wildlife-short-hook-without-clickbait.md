### Narration draft

A strong wildlife hook creates a question the footage can genuinely answer. Start with a real detail, an observable behaviour or a useful question. Tell viewers why it matters, but do not promise a rare attack, a rescue or a surprise that never appears. Match the title and thumbnail to the actual video. Then pay off the opening question with evidence, context and a clear explanation. Curiosity earns attention; accuracy earns trust. If the footage cannot support the hook, rewrite the hook.
