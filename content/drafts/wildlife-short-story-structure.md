### Narration draft

Wildlife footage becomes a story when every shot helps answer one clear question. Start by choosing one behaviour or moment you can explain honestly. Next, give viewers the context: where are we, what animal are we observing, and what should they notice? Then arrange the real footage so the detail supports the behaviour, instead of making unrelated clips look like one encounter. Finish by explaining what the evidence shows—and what it does not show.
