### Narration draft

A focused wildlife Short starts before the edit. Step one: choose one subject and one question the footage can genuinely answer. Step two: plan a beginning, a middle and a payoff. Open with the question, show the relevant context and behaviour, then explain what viewers should understand. Step three: check the evidence. Verify factual claims, confirm every asset's usage rights and make sure the sequence does not imply a different animal, location or event. If a shot is missing, change the story—not the truth.
