### Narration draft

A faster edit is not always better. Try three purposeful changes. First, remove repeated shots that add no new information. Second, vary the framing only when the footage supports it—use a wider view for context and a closer view for a meaningful detail. Third, give important behaviour enough time to register. Do not cut away just because a shot is quiet. Keep the real sequence intact, preserve useful natural sound and make every transition serve the explanation.
