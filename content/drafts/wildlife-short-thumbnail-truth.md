### Narration draft

A thumbnail should make people curious without promising a scene that never happens. Start with a clear image that genuinely appears in the video. Give the main subject room, remove clutter and use only a few words that remain readable on a small screen. Make sure the title and thumbnail tell the same story. Do not combine unrelated animals, invent an attack or use an image that suggests a moment your video never shows.
