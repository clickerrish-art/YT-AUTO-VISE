### Narration draft

A little research can help you notice behaviour without interfering with it. Start with the species and habitat, then read reliable sources about its normal activity, diet and seasonal context. Check local rules and ask a qualified guide when filming in a managed area. Turn your research into observation questions, not a script for what the animal must do. Plan a shot list, wait patiently and record what actually happens. Later, compare your notes with the footage before writing narration. Research should improve observation, never stage an encounter.
