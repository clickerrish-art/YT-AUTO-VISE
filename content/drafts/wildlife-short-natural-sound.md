### Narration draft

Sometimes the most powerful wildlife soundtrack is the real environment. Before adding music, listen to the original recording. You may hear wind, water, insects, birds or the quiet atmosphere that helps viewers understand the place. Keep useful natural sound where it is clear and relevant. Record from a safe position without approaching or disturbing animals. Balance narration so it remains understandable, and use music only when you have permission for the exact use. Never add a sound that falsely suggests an animal performed an action. This product is sold through my own Selar business; purchases generate revenue for my business.
