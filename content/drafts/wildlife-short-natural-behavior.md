### Narration draft

The best wildlife shot should never come at an animal's expense. Learn the rules for the location before filming, and follow the instructions of rangers, guides and land managers. Keep a respectful distance. Never bait, chase, corner or handle an animal to create a dramatic moment. If your presence appears to change the animal's behaviour, stop and give it more space. Protect nests, dens, habitats and sensitive location details. Your camera records what happens; it should not force what happens.
