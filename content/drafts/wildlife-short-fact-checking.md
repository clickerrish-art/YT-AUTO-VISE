### Narration draft

A dramatic voiceover is only useful when the facts stand up. Check the species name, location, date and behaviour before recording. Look for primary research, recognized scientific institutions, conservation organizations and qualified experts. Record which source supports each claim. Separate what the footage visibly shows from what you infer. A head turn does not prove an animal's emotion, and one clip cannot establish what an entire species always does. If evidence is mixed, explain the uncertainty or remove the claim. Accuracy is part of good storytelling.
