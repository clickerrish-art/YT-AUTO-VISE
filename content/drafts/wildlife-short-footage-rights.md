### Narration draft

Before publishing wildlife footage, check three separate rights areas. First, confirm who created the video and whether your permission covers the exact use you plan—including public posting and commercial promotion. Second, check music, photographs, graphics and sound effects separately; permission for one clip does not automatically cover everything inside it. Third, save the source page, licence terms, attribution wording, download record and the exact file you received. A clip being online does not make it free to reuse.
