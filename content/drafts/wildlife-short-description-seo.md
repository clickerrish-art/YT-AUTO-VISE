### Narration draft

Metadata works best when it accurately describes what viewers will learn. Put the subject and the viewer benefit near the beginning of your description. Use the main search phrase naturally, then add useful context, sources and accurate links. Do not repeat keywords just to fill space, and never promise a result the video cannot deliver. If you promote a product, disclose the relationship clearly and use the correct destination link. Finally, compare the description with the finished video before publishing. Clear metadata helps people decide whether the video is right for them. This product is sold through my own Selar business; purchases generate revenue for my business.
