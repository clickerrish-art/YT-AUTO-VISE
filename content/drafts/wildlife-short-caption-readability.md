### Narration draft

If viewers cannot read your captions on a phone, the edit is working against the story. Keep each caption short enough to read comfortably. Use strong contrast, a clear typeface and consistent placement inside safe margins. Avoid covering the animal, important action or platform controls. Break lines at natural speech pauses, and do not split names or phrases awkwardly. Most importantly, compare every caption with the final recording. Automatic transcription can make mistakes, especially with species names. Read the whole video on a phone before export.
