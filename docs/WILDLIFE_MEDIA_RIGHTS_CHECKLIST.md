# Wildlife footage rights and review checklist

This project targets SonicVhibez only. It must never publish to Nature Frontiers.

## Preferred free sources

- **NOAA Fisheries B-roll packages:** https://videos.fisheries.noaa.gov/category/videos/b-roll-packages---all
  NOAA says B-roll packages are public domain and may be downloaded and used with credit. Preserve the package's opening slate and read its attribution notes carefully: individual clips may be credited to outside organizations, with permit details. Use only a B-roll package, not an ordinary narrative video whose embedded third-party clips may not be reusable.
  Suggested attribution format: `Footage: NOAA Fisheries [exact package/clip title]; see original package credits.`
- **U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service B-roll:** https://www.fws.gov/library/collections/b-roll-videos
  The agency invites reuse with credit, but warns that some finished videos contain music licensed only for distribution of the full finished production. For Shorts assembled from excerpts, do not use the embedded music unless a separate license is verified. Prefer verified silent footage or remove the source audio and use the separately generated narration.

## Required evidence per asset

Before setting `rightsConfirmed=true`, a human must record:
1. Exact source URL and asset/package title.
2. The source's license/reuse statement and date checked.
3. The exact attribution required by the source.
4. Confirmation that third-party footage, music, logos, people/likenesses, and other restrictions have been checked.
5. A local copy of the relevant license statement or screenshot/link in the project's private records.

A source being free to view or downloadable does not by itself mean it is reusable. Do not use YouTube downloads, scraped clips, or an asset with unclear rights.

## Per-video final review

- Confirm the visual content actually matches the narration and thumbnail.
- Verify animal species and behavioural claims against reliable sources; do not imply staged or unrelated footage shows the narrated behaviour.
- Keep safe, ethical wildlife practices; no baiting, chasing, handling, or disturbance.
- Listen to the final voice track; align every caption to the actual spoken words.
- Confirm no source music remains unless licensed for this exact use.
- Keep the promotional product link exactly `https://selar.com/0u19v8u99l` and describe it clearly as a promotional resource.
- Check thumbnail truthfulness, text legibility on mobile, spelling, and 1280x720 dimensions.
- Confirm the target account is SonicVhibez and initial upload visibility is private.

## Stop conditions

The automated text-led draft is not a wildlife-footage video and must not be represented as one. Do not set `rightsConfirmed` or `complianceReviewed` to true automatically. Do not enable public publishing or scheduled public uploads. The private uploader additionally requires verified YouTube OAuth credentials and an exact channel ID match.
