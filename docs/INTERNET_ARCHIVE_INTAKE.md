**Current status — 2026-11-21: historical discovery only; no production use authorized.**

The earlier intake plan treated Internet Archive and NOAA packages as possible B-roll supplements. That plan is superseded by the current owner production policy: genuine original creator-captured wildlife media is required. Generic stock footage, archive packages, public-domain B-roll and AI-generated wildlife visuals are not acceptable substitutes.

## Current rules

- Do not download or render Internet Archive wildlife clips for production.
- Do not use Internet Archive, NOAA, Pexels, Pixabay or Wikimedia Commons footage as a substitute for original creator-captured media, even if the listing appears public domain or has a permissive licence.
- Existing discovery metadata is retained as reference/audit evidence only. It does not grant production eligibility.
- A future third-party creator's original field recording may be considered only if it is delivered by the creator/rights holder with creator identity, capture provenance, exact original source bytes, SHA-256, explicit commercial-use permission, technical review and exact-hash human approval.
- No archive or AI-generated clip may be represented as a SonicVhibez or Nature Frontiers original field recording.

## Historical discovery records

Any prior Internet Archive search results, item identifiers, metadata exports, source-page licence checks or proposed shot matches remain unapproved research leads. Do not treat source-page metadata or a download URL as proof of original capture, chain of custody, technical quality or rights to a derivative commercial documentary.
