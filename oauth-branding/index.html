SonicVhibez YouTube Automation

Tools and workflows for managing authorized video publishing operations for the SonicVhibez YouTube channel.

What this application does

SonicVhibez YouTube Automation supports channel-specific video publishing workflows, including preparing and scheduling approved video content, requesting the YouTube permissions needed to upload videos, reading channel and video information needed for operational checks, and verifying the result of an authorized publishing action.

The application is intended for the SonicVhibez channel only. It is not affiliated with or operated by Google or YouTube. It does not make content ownership or rights decisions on behalf of a user. Content must be authorized and cleared before publication.

Google and YouTube access

When the channel owner authorizes the application, Google OAuth is used to obtain the permissions requested on the consent screen. The application uses YouTube upload access for authorized video uploads and read-only access for channel/video checks. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's third-party connections page.
